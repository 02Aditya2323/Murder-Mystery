### Tier 1
Search the plot rather than a title: a Victorian stage thriller in which a husband quietly dims the gas lamps each evening and tells his wife she imagined it.

### Tier 2
That play gave the English language a word for exactly what Dr. Vane did to Mira for three years. You need the year of its London premiere and the husband's surname, run together in capitals with no space.

### Tier 3
For the second stage: the report states one time of death, and the telephone company records a call later that evening. The gap between them in minutes is the first half. The badge number is signed at the foot of the report itself.
