### Tier 1
Start with the people, not the paperwork. Every member of the E-CESA council has a public professional profile. Read the biography line on each one. Not their posts, not their job history, the bio.

### Tier 2
One of those bios carries a block of characters that is not a sentence and is not a word in any language. That is base64. Any online decoder will open it, and what comes out is a link.

### Tier 3
The link opens an image, and the image is not meant to be read with your eyes. Scan it. It takes you to a post, and the flag is written in the post.
