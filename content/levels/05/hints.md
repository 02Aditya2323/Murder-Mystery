### Tier 1
Each lie is broken by exactly one item in the registry. Not two, and not none.

### Tier 2
Start with the statements that commit to a specific time or a specific place. Those are the only claims physical evidence can touch.

### Tier 3
Not everyone is lying. One of the six makes no factual claim at all, and one person's account simply is not covered by anything on the table yet. Do not force a match where the evidence does not reach.
