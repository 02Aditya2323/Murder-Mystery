### Tier 1
Not every system in that house agrees on what time it is. One of the five is wired wrong, and it is a panel, not a computer.

### Tier 2
One physical event in the hallway was caught by two different systems at the same instant. Line those two rows up against each other and you have the panel's error, to the second.

### Tier 3
A scheduled sync is not a person. Rule out everything the machines did on a timer, find the last row a human body had to be present for, correct the panel before you read it, then apply the lower bound the surgeon gave you.
