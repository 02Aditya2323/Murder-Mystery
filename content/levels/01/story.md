> *"Trauma and body temperature put the real time of death between **45 and 75 minutes** after the last time a person was physically active in that study. The state of the clock suggests it was staged."*

- **SEC** - the house alarm panel
- **DOORBELL** - the front door camera
- **PC** - Dr Vane's desktop
- **PHONE** - the ground floor landline
- **THERMO** - the central heating

1. Every system in the house runs on **India Standard Time**. One of these five does not.
2. One of the clocks is simply **set wrong**. You can work out by how much, because one event in the hallway was caught by two different devices at the same instant.
3. Machines log themselves all night. A scheduled backup is not a person.

**What to do:** find the last moment a human being was active in that study, then add the lower end of the surgeon's window.
