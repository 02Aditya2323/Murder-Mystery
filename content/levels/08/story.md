# The Garage Panel

The front door runs off the main security panel, and that panel went quiet at 21:20: access events and alarm dispatch suppressed under a maintenance window somebody scheduled that afternoon. Its record of the night is worthless: it shows {{killer.full}} badging out at 21:18 and nobody at all after him.

The garage keypad is older and was never brought onto the main system. It runs its own relay logic on its own board, keeps its own local memory, and nobody thought about it for four years.

That board is on your table. Forensics have seated the ICs and set the input switches exactly as they were found, and **do not touch the switches**. What they could not do is finish the wiring: one jumper between two stages is missing, and the controller will not report until the circuit is whole.

**Your Objective:**
Your maintenance sheet shows the three output stages. Two of them are legible. The third is a gap, and all that survives of it is a record of how that stage behaves.

Work out which gate belongs in the gap, fit the jumper to the matching IC, and read the three output LEDs. Submit the three digits.
