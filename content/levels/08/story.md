# The Garage Panel

The front door runs off the main security panel, and that panel went quiet at 21:20 — access events and alarm dispatch suppressed under a maintenance window somebody scheduled that afternoon. Its record of the night is therefore worthless: it shows {{killer.full}} badging out at 21:18 and nobody at all after him.

The garage keypad is older and was never brought onto the main system. It runs its own relay logic on its own board, keeps its own local memory, and nobody thought about it for four years.

The board recovered from the garage is on your table.

**Your Objective:**
Your maintenance sheet carries the panel's output logic, but one gate has been smudged out — all that survives of it is a partial truth table. Work out which gate it must be, build the circuit on your kit, and find the switch pattern that lights the output LED.

There is exactly one. Submit it as four bits, in the order `A B C D`.
