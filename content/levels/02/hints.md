### Tier 1
There are only two lengths of tap. That is the whole of the information.

### Tier 2
Short is a dot, long is a dash. A short gap separates letters and a longer gap separates words, so count the gaps before you count the taps.

### Tier 3
Three words. The last of them is a name you have already seen written down in this case.
